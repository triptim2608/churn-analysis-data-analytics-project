# Ravenstack SaaS Retention Report and Churn Analysis

# Overview
A SaaS startup, RavenStack, conducted a pilot launch before going public and collected customer data across subscriptions, feature usage, support tickets, and churn events. The objective was to identify churn drivers, revenue risks, and customer retention issues before scaling the product.

# Project Objectives 
- Measure overall customer churn and retention performance.
- Identify industries, regions, and customer segments with the highest churn risk.
- Analyze key reasons behind customer departures.
- Assess the impact of product adoption and support experience on churn.
- Quantify revenue at risk and highlight accounts requiring immediate attention.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for stakeholder decision-making.

# Key insights
- 22% churn rate (110 out of 500 accounts), significantly above the healthy SaaS benchmark of 5%.
- $2.36M MRR at Risk, representing nearly 21% of total recurring revenue.
- DevTools customers showed the highest churn rate at 31%, making it the most vulnerable industry segment.
- Product features and support issues accounted for 36.3% of churn reasons, indicating internal operational challenges rather than market competition.
- Churn accelerated throughout the year, peaking in Q4, suggesting worsening customer experience over time.
- Support satisfaction remained below target, highlighting the need for service quality improvements.
- High-risk accounts were identified proactively for retention efforts before churn occurs.

  # Results
I identified the key factors driving customer churn and revenue loss at RavenStack. Through SQL analysis and Power BI dashboards, high-risk industries, countries, and customer accounts were uncovered, while MRR-at-risk was quantified to measure business impact. The insights enabled data-driven retention strategies and highlighted opportunities to improve product adoption, customer support, and overall customer satisfaction.
